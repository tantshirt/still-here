2 subdivision basemeshes, ~1k tris without subdivision, ~4k with, nice n simple.
Rigged, but the topology is not completely optimized for animation. I used these for sculpting mostly or further topo work. Maybe it can be done.
Enjoy.

Models:

Male: Lowpoly mesh, rigged, UVed (1152 tris)
Female: Lowpoly mesh, rigged, UVed (1158 tris)
